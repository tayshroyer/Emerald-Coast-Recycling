The gap between Destin and Panama City Beach

This corridor is one of the fastest-growing coastlines in the Southeast — and its recycling infrastructure hasn't kept up. The nearest full-service drop-off is a 20 to 30 minute drive for most residents. Three groups are left out entirely:

  • Condos & high-rises — most receive no recycling service at all, curbside or otherwise.
  • Townhome communities — outside city franchise zones, with nowhere close to take recyclables.
  • Vacation rentals — guests generate more packaging waste than residents, with zero infrastructure to handle it.
